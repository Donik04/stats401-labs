This site collects my lab work for STATS 401. Each lab has its own page, linked from the navigation above.

Labs

  • Lab 1 — Getting Started with D3.js HTML, CSS and JavaScript, the DOM, SVG, loading CSV and JSON, and a student score bar chart.
  • Lab 2 — Multivariate Visualization with D3 Scales, axes, legends and tooltips, plus a four-dimensional view of twelve cities with a design justification.
  • Lab 3 — Web Data Acquisition with Python Scraping 1,000 book records with requests and BeautifulSoup, shown as a sortable, filterable table.
  • Lab 4 — Cleaning Web Data for Visualization Cleaning 1,000 raw #covid19 tweets with pandas, scoring them with a RoBERTa sentiment model, and asking whether tweets typed by hand read differently from ones a scheduling tool queued.
  • Lab 5 — Interactive Network Visualization A 50-station transit network drawn twice, as a force-directed node-link diagram and as an adjacency matrix, and what each view can and cannot answer about the same 50 connections.
  • Lab 6 — Hierarchical Data: Trees and Treemaps A World → continent → area → country GDP hierarchy built from a flat CSV with Python, drawn as squarified and slice-and-dice treemaps with GDP as area and growth status as colour, plus a collapsible tree and a zoomable treemap.
  • Lab 7 — Temporal Data: Line Charts and an Animated Network Twelve companies and 27 commercial relationships animated over 60 days, with pinned positions so nodes never jump, plus two static overviews that keep the whole period on screen — and 180 days of weather as a multi-series line chart with a metric switch, brush filtering and an animated marker.
